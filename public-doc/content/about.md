Livebound is a local-first publishing workspace for CivitAI creators. It turns existing image folders into a browser-based workspace for reviewing generation metadata, preparing posts, scheduling publication, and keeping local and remote post state in sync. It runs on your machine and does not rewrite your original image files when you edit metadata.

The project is built for creator-directed publishing: you choose the images, text, metadata, resources, timing and remote actions. Optional language-model assistance can suggest content, but it is not required and does not make publishing decisions for you.

Livebound is free and open-source software under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later**.
