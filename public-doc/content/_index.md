---
title: "Livebound Documentation"
description: "User guide for Livebound, a local-first publishing workspace for CivitAI creators."
---

Livebound turns existing image folders into a local browser-based workflow for reviewing generation data, preparing posts, scheduling publication, and keeping local post state aligned with CivitAI.

Your original image files stay where they are. Metadata edits are stored in Livebound and applied to temporary upload copies when a CivitAI operation needs them.
